The audit universe is the configured map of everything your department can audit. Engagements are against units in the universe, and what each engagement learns flows back into its parent unit.

## Universe Categories and Auditable Units

Auditable units are the overarching data structure storing information about an entity that can be audited. Each unit carries:

- Its **Universe Category** and **Audit Type**: where it sits in the universe and what kind of audit work it receives.
- The **Enterprise Risks** flagged as relevant to it.
- Its **Process Areas**, **Risks** and **Relevant Controls**:  retained across engagements, so the next audit of this unit starts from a known map instead of a blank page.
- Its **engagement history**,  every audit that has covered the audit is listed as referenced under the unit.

## Enterprise Risks

Enterprise risks are typically a **configuration**: a fixed list from your organization's ERM function. They are flagged on each engagement they apply to, connecting audit work to the enterprise risk register. The engagement's own risk assessment happens in its [Risk Matrix](/engagement#lifecycle), whose rows are engagement-specific risk records.

## Focus Areas

Focus areas are organization-defined lenses configured alongside enterprise risks. Where your methodology requires it, each engagement relates to both enterprise risks **and** focus areas. 

## Audit Types

Audit types classify the kind of audit work across the universe. The list is yours to configure. 

**For example:**

| Code | Audit Type |
| --- | --- |
| FX | Financial Statement |
| FO | Financial Operational |
| CR | Compliance / Regulatory |
| IT | Info Security |
| FD | Fiduciary - decisions made by people with fiduciary responsibility |
