The audit universe is the configured map of everything your department could audit. It's what makes Recap IA risk-based: engagements are scoped from the universe, and what each engagement learns flows back into it.

## Universe Categories and Auditable Units

The universe is a two-tier taxonomy: **Universe Categories** (e.g. Expense, Revenue) at the top, with **Auditable Units** filed under them. Controls carry a Function; a control's category follows from the unit it belongs to — one taxonomy, one source of truth.

Auditable units are the overarching data structure storing information about an entity that can be audited. Each unit carries:

- Its **Universe Category** and **Audit Type** — where it sits in the universe and what kind of audit work it receives.
- The **Enterprise Risks** flagged as relevant to it.
- Its **Process Areas** and **Relevant Controls** — retained across engagements, so the next audit of this unit starts from a known map instead of a blank page.
- Its **engagement history** — every audit that has covered it.

This is what makes Recap IA cumulative: units remember, engagements borrow.

## Enterprise Risks

Enterprise risks are **configuration, not working data**: a fixed list from your organization's ERM function. They are flagged on each engagement they apply to, connecting audit work to the enterprise risk register. The engagement's own risk assessment happens in its [Risk Matrix](/engagement#lifecycle), whose rows are engagement-specific risk records.

## Focus Areas

Focus areas are organization-defined lenses configured alongside enterprise risks. Where your methodology requires it, each engagement relates to both an enterprise risk **and** a focus area — giving leadership a second axis for seeing coverage.

## Audit Types

Audit types classify the kind of audit work across the universe. The list is yours to configure — for example:

| Code | Audit Type |
| --- | --- |
| FX | Financial Statement |
| FO | Financial Operational |
| CR | Compliance / Regulatory |
| IT | Info Security |
| FD | Fiduciary — decisions made by people with fiduciary responsibility |
