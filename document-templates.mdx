**Recap IA uses three documents in engagements:** 

- Planning Memos
- Workpapers
- Audit Reports 

Each is initially generated from case data and provided as an artifact for auditors to review, adjust and complete. Recap's integrations help information flow in and out of the application without being manually copy-pasted. 

## Planning Memo

Produced at the end of the Planning stage, once the engagement plan has been built and approved. CAE are able to export the plan to a formatted PDF for easy distribution to auditees. 

## Workpaper

One Excel workpaper per Test. When a test is created, Recap IA copies a blank workpaper template into the test's SharePoint folder as `{Test Name} Workpaper.xlsx` and pre-fills its header from fields in the **Test** case that auditors draft as a part of their work program:

| Workpaper section | Pre-filled by system |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Engagement name, test name, period of testing |
| Purpose | Test objective |
| Population | Population description |
| Sampling approach | Sampling approach |
| Testing procedures | Testing procedures |

Auditors then complete the sample testing in Excel. See [SharePoint for Fieldwork](/sharepoint-fieldwork) for how access and review work.

## Audit Report

The report deck is generated in the Reporting stage using all the information accumulated throughout the engagement.  The branded PowerPoint follows the standard structure:

1. Title
2. Executive summary, conclusions and contents
3. Internal audit approach
4. Observations - one slide per observation, with risk rating (Low / Medium / High), recommendation, management response, owner and due date
5. Appendix: risk rating definitions
