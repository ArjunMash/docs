title: "Document Templates"
description: "The three documents every engagement produces, and how Recap IA generates them"
---

Recap IA uses three documents in engagements: **Planning Memos**, **Workpapers**, and **Audit Reports**. Each is generated from case data — the same facts flow from planning through fieldwork into the report, instead of being copy-pasted between Office files.

## Planning Memo

Produced at the end of the Planning stage, once the CAE approves scope. The memo captures the engagement's approach and the risks identified during planning, generated as a Word document and rendered to PDF for review and distribution to management.

## Workpaper

One Excel workpaper per Test. When a test is created, Recap IA copies a blank workpaper template into the test's SharePoint folder as `{Test Name} Workpaper.xlsx` and pre-fills its header from the test case:

| Workpaper section | Pre-filled from the Test case |
| --- | --- |
| Banner | Engagement name, test name, period of testing |
| Purpose | Test objective |
| Population | Population description |
| Sampling approach | Sampling approach |
| Testing procedures | Testing procedures |

Auditors then complete the sample testing in Excel. See [SharePoint for Fieldwork](/sharepoint-fieldwork) for how access and review work.

## Audit Report

The report deck is generated in the Reporting stage from the engagement's observations — a branded PowerPoint following the standard structure:

1. Title
2. Executive summary, conclusions and contents
3. Internal audit approach
4. Observations — one slide per observation, with risk rating (Low / Medium / High), recommendation, management response, owner and due date
5. Appendix: risk rating definitions
