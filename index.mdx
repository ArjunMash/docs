Recap IA is a tool to manage documentation for Internal Audit teams running risk-based engagements in line with IIA standards.

## Getting Started

At its core Recap IA is object oriented, meaning that each piece of data you interact with is related, whether that be an engagement, risk, control or anything else. As such, understanding Recap IA's data model is essential to understanding how your data is tied together.
