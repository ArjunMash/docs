---
title: "Auditors and Managers"
description: "The personas in Recap IA and what each can do"
---

Recap IA has a small, deliberate set of personas — built for lean audit departments, not a permissions matrix that takes a quarter to configure.

| Persona | Role in an engagement |
| --- | --- |
| **Auditor** | Assigned to tests. Executes fieldwork, completes workpapers (with edit access to their own workpapers during fieldwork), documents observations, and submits tests for review. |
| **Audit Manager** | The reviewer. Approves engagement scope and planning memos, reviews submitted workpapers (view + comment), approves each test's observations, and signs off on draft reports. This persona acts as the CAE — the delegation of CAE approvals to the engagement's reviewer is a documented methodology decision. |
| **Auditee / Process Owner** | The business side. Fulfills evidence requests and owns action items through remediation. |

<Info>
  Tests can have multiple assigned auditors, but a single reviewer. When a test is submitted for review, the auditors' edit access to the workpaper is withdrawn and the reviewer receives comment access — so the record under review can't change mid-review.
</Info>
