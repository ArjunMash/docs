---
title: "Auditees"
---

Engagements, action items and evidence requests all reference auditees. Given that auditees tend to be consistent over time RecapIA also maintains a ledger of Name, e-mail and title of auditees. 

This allows auditees that are added to this ledger to be easily pulled and tagged on engagements or requests. It is also what supports automated email notifications through Recap IA.
